Contact Support

How to reach us, what to include in a report, and where to discuss with other users.

Ways to reach us

  • Feedback & Suggestions — in the panel's More menu or the manager's Features menu. It signs you in automatically to our feedback community where you can post and vote on ideas.
  • Email — service@webnoteshub.com for anything private (billing, account issues, data questions).

What to include

A great report gets the problem fixed faster. When possible, add:

  • The page URL where the problem happened.
  • What you expected vs. what happened, and the steps to reproduce.
  • A screenshot if the issue is visible.
  • Your extension version — shown at the bottom of the extension popup.
  • For sync or payment questions: your account email and roughly when the issue occurred.

Billing and orders

Upgrades, plan purchases, and the 14-day refund window are handled in the manager under Orders. For payment questions that need a human, email us with your account email and the order date — we will take it from there.
