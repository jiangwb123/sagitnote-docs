Text Highlights

Everything about marking text on a page: colors, styles, notes, tags, auto mode, and how highlights survive page changes.

Creating a highlight

Select any text on the page — a color bar appears next to the selection. Click a color dot and the text is highlighted immediately, using the style currently selected in the panel's style group. Right after that you can:

  • Click the lit-up pen button in the bar to attach a note and tags to the fresh highlight, and choose how it is displayed: as a hover tooltip, or always visible on the page.
  • Want a different color? Click the highlight itself and restyle it in the quick popup.

After one highlight, just select the next sentence — the bar appears for every selection, so annotating a whole passage is quick.

The five highlight styles

StyleEffect
FillClassic marker behind the text.
BorderRectangle outline around the text.
UnderlineStraight line under the text.
WavyWavy line under the text — handy for “revisit this”.
Text ColorRecolors the characters themselves.

Line width does not affect highlights — it only applies to drawing tools.

Colors

The palette has six colors: red, amber, green, blue, violet, orange — all available to everyone. You can rename colors in Settings (Palette section, up to 12 characters) — for example to mark “follow-up” or “quote” — turning your colors into a personal classification system.

Auto mode

Turn on Auto on the panel and simply keep selecting text: every selection is highlighted automatically with the current color — no clicks needed. Auto can't be combined with the drawing tools; picking a tool turns Auto off. The setting is remembered per browser.

Editing and deleting

  • Quick popup — click an existing highlight: change its color, open the annotation editor (pen), or delete it (trash).
  • Annotation Detail — open Browse (the count button), click the pencil icon on its card: type a note, manage tags, change color and style, or delete.
  • Manager — in the Annotation Manager, you can edit any highlight's note and tags from its card.

Highlights that carry a note behave like every annotation: close the note popup with its ✕ button, and a small bubble icon stays right after the highlight — click it to bring the note back. See Annotation popups.

How highlights survive

Highlights are anchored to the text they mark and are re-applied automatically when you revisit the page. Sagitnote is resilient: if the page has shifted slightly, several fallback strategies relocate your highlights, and minor page changes heal on their own. Tracking parameters such as utm are ignored when matching pages, so highlights follow the article no matter which link you opened it from. To help highlights stick:

  • Highlight stable content — articles, documentation, posts.
  • On heavily dynamic pages (feeds, virtualized lists), a highlight may occasionally fail to re-locate; simply re-apply it.
