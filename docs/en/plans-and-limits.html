ℹ
No account? Local mode

You can annotate without signing in — data stays on this device only. Limits match the Free plan (100 new annotations per day, 50,000 total, etc.), but cloud sync, sharing, snapshots, and image upload are off — export your data before switching devices or clearing the browser. Signing in with a free account turns all of those on.

Checking your usage

Click your name in the manager header: the user panel shows how much of your plan's annotation and document limits you have used, plus a cloud storage bar (images, snapshots, and documents share the same pool).

What happens at a limit

  • When you hit a per-page or per-day limit, a message explains why the annotation wasn't created.
  • When the free total-annotation limit is reached, new annotations are still saved locally — upload to the cloud pauses until you're back under the limit. You can still edit and delete existing annotations.
  • Items that cannot be uploaded (for example, an oversized snapshot) appear in the sync failures viewer with the reason — nothing is lost, the data stays local.

Upgrading

Open it from Go Pro in the manager header, Upgrade Pro in the panel's More menu or the popup, or any upgrade prompt. The upgrade panel shows your current plan, the plans you can buy with prices and durations, and a buy-again preview (time from a repeat purchase is added to your current expiry date). Payment is handled by the checkout page; orders — including the 14-day refund window — are available in the manager under Orders.
